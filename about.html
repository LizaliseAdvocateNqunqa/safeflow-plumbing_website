WHO WE ARE

About SafeFlow

SafeFlow Plumbing Services

SafeFlow Plumbing Services is a plumbing company founded in February 2023 by two individuals, Sonwabise and Lizalise.

Our company is based in Mthatha, Eastern Cape. We operate around Mthatha and its surrounding townships.

We believe that good plumbing is about more than simply fixing pipes. It is about providing dependable service, quality workmanship and solutions that customers can trust.

Our team is committed to helping homes, businesses and other properties with their plumbing needs.

Founded: February 2023
Founders: Sonwabise & Lizalise
Based: Mthatha, Eastern Cape
